Mekong Sand Watch — Optical Validation & Labeling GUI

Verify Sentinel-1 radar detections against optical satellite imagery (PlanetScope / Sentinel-2 / Google Earth)
Local only
1 Pick a radar target: Start with the orange Stationary Hotspots (O007–O009). Each dot is a bright Sentinel-1 radar reflection.
2 Open optical view on that date: Click PlanetScope (3m), Copernicus (10m S2), or Google Earth to inspect the river on the radar dates.
3 Label what you see: Press 1 Confirmed vessel/barge, 0 False positive, or U Unclear/clouded, then N for Next.

Shortcuts: 1 / 0 / U to label, N / P to navigate. Saves automatically in browser.
1 (Confirmed): Boat or barge visible at/near pin on closest cloud-free optical date.
0 (False Positive): Empty water, riverbank clutter, bridge sidelobe, or permanent structure.
U (Unclear): Heavy clouds or ambiguous at 10 m optical resolution.
